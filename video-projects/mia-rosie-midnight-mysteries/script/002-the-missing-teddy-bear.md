# The Missing Teddy Bear

- **Project:** mia-rosie-midnight-mysteries

- **Format:** vertical, 6 clips x 10 s = about 60 s

## Angle

**Promise:** Mia's favorite teddy bear is missing, and she and Rosie follow a trail of golden sparkles to a ribbon on the toy shelf and a bump under the blanket, where Teddy has been kept warm all along.

**Why it's uncommon:** The story and dialogue are supplied by the owner (Episode 2 of Season 1) and are used word for word; the angle was not re-invented. The craft here is keeping the same two characters, the same bedroom and one visible change per scene (a missing bear, a sparkle, a ribbon, a bump, a surprise, a hug). Every landmark (toy shelf, toy chest, window, bed) is shown in the first scene, Teddy and the ribbon each keep one look, and each scene ends where the next begins.

**Obvious versions rejected:** Not applicable: the owner's episode script was followed as written.

## Look anchors

- **Mia:** Mia, a 7-year-old girl with long wavy dark-brown hair, big warm-brown eyes and rosy cheeks, in light pink floral pajamas and pink bunny slippers
- **Rosie:** Rosie, a vintage rag doll with long wavy dark-brown hair under a large crimson bow, black X-stitched button eyes, rosy cheeks, a small stitched mouth, a crimson dress with a white round collar, white-and-red striped stockings and black Mary Jane shoes
- **Bedroom:** Mia's bedroom, a cozy room with a lavender canopy bed under a pink floral blanket and purple star-pattern quilt, an arched moonlit window with white curtains, a dark wooden toy shelf holding storybooks and a golden carousel music box beside the window, a wooden toy chest with brass corners and a curved lid at the foot of the bed, and a tall white closet with a round brass knob
- **Plush bear:** Teddy, a plush tan teddy bear with soft round ears and a stitched smile
- **Sparkle trail:** a thin trail of tiny glowing golden sparkles
- **Ribbon:** a small blue satin ribbon
