## Angle

**Promise:** Mia hears a tiny voice call her name at midnight, finds that her doll Rosie is talking, and together they follow a golden glow in the toy chest to a glowing star that belongs to their friendship.

**Why it's uncommon:** The story and dialogue are supplied by the owner (Episode 1 of Season 1) and are used word for word; the angle was not re-invented. The craft here is keeping the same two characters, the same bedroom and one visible change per scene (a voice, a tilt, a glow, a star, a promise).

**Obvious versions rejected:** Not applicable: the owner's episode script was followed as written.
