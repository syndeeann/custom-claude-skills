- **Project:** mia-rosie-midnight-mysteries

## Angle

**Promise:** Mia and Rosie follow strange patches of moonlight across the bedroom floor and watch them become a star, then a heart, when Mia moves the curtain.

**Why it's uncommon:** The story and dialogue are supplied by the owner (Episode 3 of Season 1) and are used word for word; the angle was not re-invented. The craft here is keeping the same two characters, the same bedroom and one visible change per scene (strange shapes, a puzzle, a star, a change, a heart, a goodnight). Every landmark (toy shelf, toy chest, window, bed) is shown in the first scene, the star and the heart each appear once and keep one look, and each scene ends where the next begins.

**Obvious versions rejected:** Not applicable: the owner's episode script was followed as written.

- **Mia:** Mia, a 7-year-old girl with long wavy dark-brown hair, big warm-brown eyes and rosy cheeks, in light pink floral pajamas and pink bunny slippers
