# The Doll That Whispered at Midnight

- **Project:** mia-rosie-midnight-mysteries

## Angle

**Promise:** A girl hears her doll whisper her name at midnight, but the doll's sewn mouth never moves, so the whole episode turns on one question: if the whisper is not Rosie's, whose is it, and why is the doll the only one watching the door?

**Why it's uncommon:** Instead of a doll that jumps out, the doll is the clue. The governing rule is "her mouth never moves": every beat shows it, so the viewer works out that Rosie is the guardian and the real voice is in the hallway. The scare is a hush and a tall shadow, never a monster, and the last image reframes Rosie as protector while leaving the hallway voice unsolved for Day 2 onward.

**Obvious versions rejected:**
- The killer doll who comes alive and jumps at the camera.
- A whispering doll that turns out to be a dream.
- A ghost child possessing the doll who says "play with me".
- Mia screams, parents arrive, nothing is there.
- The doll moves closer each time the camera cuts away.

Other angles considered and killed: the doll is a ventriloquist for the house (guessable by clip 2); the whisper is Mia's own voice (saved for Day 3); the music box speaks (saved for Day 5); a doll that counts down to midnight (swap test failed, works for any toy).

## Full narration

Midnight. Mia heard a whisper say her name. It came from her doll, but the doll's stitched mouth never moved. She tiptoed over. Rosie now faced the wall, and nobody in the house had turned her. Up close, it pleaded: keep your eyes open. Black buttons gleamed, yet the sewn smile stayed perfectly shut. Then a shadow slid across the doorway, tall and silent, one long hand lifting. A blink later, nothing. Morning found them asleep, the doll smiling. But the voice was never hers. Out in the hall, someone still murmured.
