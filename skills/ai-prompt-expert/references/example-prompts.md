## Clips

### Clip 01 — HOOK (8s)
- **Narration:** Every Tuesday at ten past four, someone collects umbrella 112. Its owner died six years ago.
- **Mode:** voiceover
- **Covers:** "Every Tuesday at ten past four" → round wall clock above a rain-streaked window with its hands just past four; "someone collects umbrella 112" → umbrella 112 hangs from a steel hook swaying as if brushed by something unseen; "Its owner died six years ago" → thick grey dust coats the shelf beneath
- **Prompt:** Low-angle locked-off shot with a slow push-in, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field. Umbrella 112, a black umbrella with a worn wooden crook handle and a yellow paper tag on a string, hangs from a steel hook, swaying as if brushed by something unseen. Thick grey dust coats the shelf beneath. Above, a round wall clock holds its hands just past four beside a rain-streaked window. Green-tinted fluorescent glow, cold and flat, with a faint haze. Ambient sound: steady drizzle, an electrical buzz, the squeak of metal. Clean frame, free of subtitles, captions, watermarks and logos.
- **Review:** The umbrella sways without any hand in shot; nobody on screen; no text on the clock face.

- **Covers:** "I run lost property at the night terminal" → Marisol behind the lost-property counter in the empty night bus terminal; "Nothing leaves without a signature" → her hands squaring the ledger's edge against the counter's rim
- **Prompt:** Wide over-the-shoulder shot, handheld, 28mm lens. Marisol, a woman in her fifties with short silver-streaked hair, reading glasses on a beaded chain and a grey wool cardigan, seen from behind, stands at a lost-property counter in an empty night bus terminal under green-tinted fluorescent tubes, with steel shelves of numbered umbrellas and a brown cloth ledger; her hands square the ledger's edge against the counter's rim. Dim aisles stretch away behind the glass. Flat green wash, cool shadows. Ambient sound: a ticking vent, distant bus brakes, paper sliding. Clean frame, free of subtitles, captions, watermarks and logos.

### Clip 03 — PATTERN (8s)
- **Narration:** Three hundred and twelve claims, one a week. The camera never catches a hand.
- **Mode:** voiceover
- **Covers:** "Three hundred and twelve claims, one a week" → long column of handwritten ledger lines, one entry for each week; "The camera never catches a hand" → ceiling security camera angled at the empty counter
- **Prompt:** Overhead shot, locked-off, 50mm lens. A long column of handwritten ledger lines runs down the page, one entry for each week, as a thumb slides along the margin and stops at the last one. In the top corner, a ceiling security camera angled at the empty counter holds a steady red dot. Cold green fluorescent wash, fibre texture visible. Ambient sound: dry rasp, a low electrical buzz, a faint ticking. Clean frame, free of subtitles, captions, watermarks and logos.
- **Review:** Handwriting stays illegible and soft; only a thumb, no face; the camera doesn't show a screen.

### Clip 04 — TURN (8s)
- **Narration:** At four o'clock a name appears in the ledger by itself. Mr. Alcántara. My oldest regular.
- **Mode:** voiceover
- **Covers:** "At four o'clock a name appears in the ledger by itself" → fountain pen untouched beside the open ledger while ink forms across a fresh line; "Mr. Alcántara. My oldest regular" → worn felt hat and steel thermos on a plastic waiting bench
- **Prompt:** Close-up, locked-off, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, focus drifting slowly to the far end of the terminal. A fountain pen lies untouched beside the open ledger while looping blue-black ink forms across a fresh line. Further back, a worn felt hat and a steel thermos rest on a plastic waiting bench by the departure doors. Cold green fluorescent glow, soft shadows. Ambient sound: the scratch of a nib, a low hum, a bus idling outside. Clean frame, free of subtitles, captions, watermarks and logos.
- **Review:** Ink forms without letters that read as words; hat and thermos stay still on the bench.

### Clip 05 — ESCALATION (8s)
- **Narration:** No signature, no exit. So I never signed. I held the hook with both hands.
- **Mode:** voiceover
- **Covers:** "No signature, no exit" → umbrella straining toward the dark glass doors; "So I never signed" → pen stays capped beside the ledger; "I held the hook with both hands" → her hands gripping a steel hook with white knuckles
- **Prompt:** Tight handheld close-up of her hands, 35mm lens. White knuckles grip a steel hook while umbrella 112, a black umbrella with a worn wooden crook handle and a yellow paper tag on a string, strains on it toward the dark glass doors, its tag whipping in a cold draught. The pen stays capped beside the ledger. Green fluorescent hum flickers once. Ambient sound: groaning metal, shallow breath, a distant engine. Clean frame, free of subtitles, captions, watermarks and logos.
- **Review:** Hands only, five fingers each, no face; the umbrella tugs without anyone else in shot.

### Clip 06 — PAYOFF (8s)
- **Narration:** Mr. Alcántara rode home safe. Umbrella 113 hangs on my hook now. Tagged with my name.
- **Mode:** voiceover
- **Covers:** "Mr. Alcántara rode home safe" → pale grey dawn light through the glass doors as a bus pulling away; "Umbrella 113 hangs on my hook now" → a second umbrella identical to the first hangs on the neighbouring hook; "Tagged with my name" → its paper tag turning in the draught
- **Prompt:** Locked-off wide shot, 35mm lens, slow pull-back at dawn. Marisol, a woman in her fifties with short silver-streaked hair, reading glasses on a beaded chain and a grey wool cardigan, seen from behind, sits at a lost-property counter in an empty night bus terminal under green-tinted fluorescent tubes, with steel shelves of numbered umbrellas and a brown cloth ledger; her hands rest folded. Pale grey light seeps through the glass doors as a bus pulling away leaves the kerb. A second umbrella, identical to the first, hangs on the neighbouring hook, its paper tag turning in the draught. Ambient sound: metal ticking softly, an engine fading, a long exhale. Clean frame, free of subtitles, captions, watermarks and logos.
- **Review:** She stays seen from behind with hands folded; the second umbrella matches the first.
